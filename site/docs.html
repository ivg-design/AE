IVG Toolkit documentation

Documentation for 35 free IVG Toolkit After Effects scripts. Browse setup, controls, examples, requirements, and individual JSX downloads.

Choose a script by the task you need to complete. Each guide includes usage instructions and a direct script download.

Animation & Rigging

  • 2-3 IK Rigger — Turns a set of existing layers into a working 2- or 3-segment IK chain.
  • CamBot — Build a fully-rigged 3D camera you steer from one panel — dial from free-fly to locked tracking, blend multiple targets, and play 26 procedural moves off a single slider.
  • Limb-a-tron — Rig a two-segment IK/FK limb in seconds — drag one null to bend it, tune everything from one Limb Control effect, and bake it when done.
  • Linearizer — Drive existing keyframed animation from any slider, position, or rotation — scrubbing the driver scrubs the animation.
  • PathMaster — Animate a layer's Position along any shape-layer path — retime, re-ease, trim, loop or ping-pong it afterwards from a single layer marker.

Composition

  • Guiderator — Drop exact ruler guides by typing a number — or a formula like 1920/2 — into a dockable panel, then click H or V.
  • Slidotron_16x9 — Build a horizontal slide-reveal rig in one click — two nested 4K comps, mirrored alpha mattes, and one 0–100 "CTRL" slider plays the transition.
  • Slidotron_9x16 — Build a vertical 9:16 slide-reveal rig in one click — two nested 1080×1920 comps, mirrored mattes, and one 0–100 "CTRL" slider plays the transition.
  • Verticalizer — One click turns a landscape clip into a rigged, resolution-agnostic split-screen portrait comp — each window framed, styled, and timed independently, with a keyframeable morph into picture-in-picture.

Effects & Audio

  • BurstMate — Drop a fully rigged radial stroke burst into your comp — every color, length, density, and dash tuned from one StrokeBurst control panel.
  • ChromaBlenderizer — Ramp a color range across selected fills, strokes, and effect colors — pick Start/End, then apply a linear blend or random scatter in one click.
  • sfxMaster — Mix Voice Over, Sound Effects, and Music from three master sliders — audio layers auto-categorized by marker comments and wired in one pass.
  • Sync-o-tron — Make any property audio-reactive in one click — TuneSync builds the Easing/Min/Max controls and wires them to the bundled Audio Reactor template.

Keyframes

  • Elast-o-matic — Add elastic overshoot or gravity bounce after any keyframe — hold Cmd/Ctrl at launch to switch variants, tune everything in Effect Controls.
  • KeyBot — Batch-edit selected keyframe values with =, +, −, ×, ÷ per axis — timing, easing, and interpolation stay untouched.
  • KeyCloneMatic — Repeat a hand-tuned keyframe set down the timeline at a fixed or decaying interval — values, easing, and spatial tangents cloned faithfully.
  • Valuatron — Stamp a keyframe at the playhead for every selected property at once — capturing current values, expressions included — in one undo.

Layers

  • Controllerizer — Gather any selected properties from any layers onto one Controller Null as matching expression controls, pre-set to current values.
  • NullBot — Parent a group of layers to one master null at their average position — move, scale, or rotate the whole group from a single handle.
  • Opac-o-bot — Parenting for Opacity — selected layers' Opacity follows their parent via expression, the one property AE parenting ignores.
  • OrderMaster — Reverse or shuffle the stacking order of a contiguous run of selected layers, or the contiguous shape groups inside one shape layer.
  • Parent-o-bot — Blend a layer's Position, Scale, Rotation, and Opacity toward up to two parent layers, each with its own 0–100% influence slider.
  • Rectangulator — Convert a parametric rectangle into a bezier shape with independent per-corner rounding and a 9-point anchor grid, driven by one effect.
  • Reseterator — Zero the transform origin of selected layers — layer anchor to [0,0,0], every nested shape-group anchor and position to [0,0].
  • SubtitleForge — Build a full caption rig in one run — text plus a self-sizing box with stroke and drop shadow, all driven from one Subtitle Setup effect.
  • TextMate — Type a word and highlight every case-sensitive occurrence of it in a text layer, each with its own Range Selector on a new animator.
  • TimeWarp-a-tron — Drive a layer's Time Remap from a 0–100 slider that scrubs a user-defined in/out frame range, framerate-correct and keyframe-free.

Paths & Shapes

  • Centralizer — Drop center and bounding-box guides around anything — one shape, a multiselected group of shapes, text, footage or a precomp.
  • Distributron — Spread a comp's layers evenly along a shape path, each auto-rotating to follow the curve, all steered live from one CTRL null.
  • Originator — Recenter a shape layer's bezier path origin on (0,0) so it scales and rotates predictably — instantly for one path, via a picker for several.
  • Trace-o-matic — Convert a layer's masks into a new shape layer with hold-keyframed paths and opacity, all styled at once by five master fill/stroke controls.
  • VertexMaster — Turn a bezier path's vertices and tangents into null-object handles you can keyframe and parent, or flip to Follow mode to track an animated path.

Utilities

  • Iteratron — Spread a property evenly across a layer stack — link a start value and an end value, and every layer in between gets its interpolated step.
  • RandoMatic — Replace every selected keyframe's value with a fresh uniform-random number inside a per-axis range you type in — timing and easing untouched.
  • Triminator — Add a Trim Paths effect — pre-keyframed 0→100% for an instant reveal — to a shape layer's contents or to the specific shape groups you select.
